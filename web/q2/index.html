Temporal result

Means peak in December–February and fall from April through August. December is highest (~1.68×10⁻⁴ mol/m²); May is lowest (~2.53×10⁻⁵ mol/m²).

Reading tip

Lower monsoon values partly reflect fewer clear scenes after cloud_fraction ≤ 0.2 masking, not only cleaner air.

How to read this page

  • NO₂ here is a tropospheric column, not roadside personal exposure.
  • P75 = top quarter of city pixels — a transparent “relatively high” rule.
  • Winter highs vs monsoon lows should be read with data availability in mind.

Spatial pattern and interpretation

Spatial result

Higher 2020 mean tropospheric NO₂ concentrates over denser built-up / traffic-intensive parts of Greater Mumbai. On Sentinel-5P’s coarse ~3.5–7 km grid, the P75 mask forms a contiguous “relatively high” core rather than street-level hotspots.

Exposure result

Roughly one-third of modelled residents aged 65+ fall inside that high-NO₂ zone. Because WorldPop is a residential surface, the share reflects where older residents are estimated to live relative to the annual NO₂ field, not individual exposure histories.

Temporal result

The monthly series shows a clear winter peak and monsoon trough under a fixed colour scale. Treat monsoon dips cautiously: cloud filtering reduces usable observations in those months.

Method, sources and limitations

Uses tropospheric_NO2_column_number_density from COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_NO2 (2020), masks cloud_fraction > 0.2, and averages annually/monthly inside one Greater Mumbai ULB boundary. WorldPop pop_age_sex_cons_unadj (India, 2020) age bands ≥ 65 are summed; high-NO₂ pixels are ≥ city-wide 75th percentile.

Spatial resolution

Sentinel-5P pixels (~3.5–7 km) cannot resolve neighbourhood or roadside differences.

Column, not personal exposure

NO₂ here is a tropospheric column density, not ground concentration or individual exposure.

Cloud / monsoon gaps

Cloud masking and monsoon cover reduce valid observations, especially June–August.

Modelled population

WorldPop age–sex counts are modelled residential estimates at ~100 m, not census microdata.
