About me

I am Aditi Saha, studying for a Master of Public Policy at IIT Tirupati. This page presents my coursework for Urban Informatics and Spatial Analytics.

I am interested in how spatial data can support everyday campus and city planning questions, starting with something visible on the ground: where waste bins are placed and how they are used.

About the project

This assignment builds an interactive web map of waste-bin observations collected on the IIT Tirupati campus using the QField app. Each observation is a point with a location, field note, time, and photograph.

The final map is made with Folium and hosted online so anyone can explore the points in a browser. The photographs are stored with the website, so they remain visible after hosting and not only on my local computer.

What I did

  • Collected waste-bin points around campus with QField.
  • Read the GeoPackage in Python with GeoPandas and checked the CRS.
  • Joined each point to its photograph using the QField attachment names.
  • Built an interactive Folium map with tooltips, popups, Streets and Satellite layers, a legend, scale bar, and north arrow.
  • Named myself as the collector on every popup and hosted the map on this webpage.

Future scope

  • Colour points by segregation status (dry, wet, not segregated) to show patterns across hostels and academic areas.
  • Compare bin coverage between residential and academic zones.
  • Repeat the survey later in the semester to see whether bins are added, moved, or better maintained.
