Spatial pattern and interpretation

Spatial. Higher NO₂ concentrates over denser built-up and traffic areas. The P75 mask forms a coarse high core because Sentinel-5P pixels are about 3.5–7 km across — useful for city-scale pattern, not neighbourhood detail.

Exposure. About one in three modelled residents aged 65+ live inside that high-NO₂ zone. The count is residential location at ~100 m WorldPop cells, not personal or outdoor exposure for any individual.

Temporal. On a fixed colour scale the year shows a clear winter peak and monsoon trough. Monsoon dips partly reflect fewer usable scenes after cloud masking, not only cleaner air.

Method, sources and limitations

Sentinel-5P OFFL L3 tropospheric NO₂ for 2020, filtered to cloud_fraction ≤ 0.2, clipped to the Greater Mumbai ULB boundary, then averaged annually and monthly. WorldPop India 2020 age–sex bands ≥ 65 are summed inside the P75 high-NO₂ mask.

Spatial resolution

~3.5–7 km S5P pixels cannot show street-level or neighbourhood detail.

Column ≠ personal exposure

Tropospheric column density is not ground-level or personal exposure.

Cloud gaps

Monsoon months retain fewer clear scenes after cloud_fraction masking.

Modelled population

WorldPop ~100 m residential estimates, not census microdata.
