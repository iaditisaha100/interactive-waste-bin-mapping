Discussion

Spatial pattern. Within Greater Mumbai, higher 2020 mean tropospheric NO₂ tends to concentrate over denser built-up and traffic-intensive parts of the ULB, while lower values appear toward less intensely urbanised edges. The P75 mask therefore highlights a contiguous “relatively high” core rather than scattered outliers, which is expected for a coarse ~3.5–7 km Sentinel-5P grid.

Temporal pattern. The monthly GIF / slider holds the colour scale fixed, so month-to-month shifts reflect changes in the cloud-masked mean column, not a changing legend. Winter months (especially Dec–Feb) show stronger city-wide means; monsoon months look quieter, partly because cloud filtering removes many scenes. Interpret month differences alongside observation availability.

Exposure reading. About 32.6% of WorldPop residents aged 65+ (~2.38 lakh of ~7.32 lakh) live in pixels at or above the city-wide 75th percentile of 2020 mean tropospheric NO₂. That is a transparent, city-specific definition of “relatively high” satellite-observed NO₂. It is not a health standard and must not be read as individual or ground-level personal exposure.

Limitations.

  • Sentinel-5P NO₂ is a tropospheric column density, not roadside concentration.
  • Spatial resolution (~3.5–7 km) smooths neighbourhood differences inside Mumbai.
  • Cloud fraction masking and monsoon cloud cover reduce valid observations in some months.
  • WorldPop 2020 age–sex surfaces are modelled residential estimates at ~100 m.
  • ULB boundary extents can differ slightly from other official GIS layers.

Methods (short)

  • Load Greater Mumbai ULB GeoJSON → GeoDataFrame → Earth Engine feature.
  • Filter S5P OFFL L3 NO₂ to 2020 and the city; mask cloud_fraction > 0.2; mean the tropospheric NO₂ band.
  • Build 12 monthly means with the same mask and colour scale; export PNG frames, GIF, and HTML slider.
  • Sum WorldPop M_*/F_* bands with age ≥ 65; compute city P75 of annual NO₂; mask high zone; sum 65+ population inside/outside.
