Discussion

Spatial

Higher NO₂ over denser built-up/traffic areas; P75 forms a coarse high core (~3.5–7 km pixels).

Exposure

~1/3 of modelled 65+ residents live in the high-NO₂ zone — residential location, not personal exposure.

Temporal

Winter peak, monsoon trough on a fixed scale; cloud masking reduces usable monsoon scenes.

Methods, sources & limitations

S5P OFFL L3 NO₂ (2020), cloud_fraction ≤ 0.2, Greater Mumbai ULB boundary. WorldPop India 2020 age bands ≥ 65; high zone = city P75.

Resolution

~3.5–7 km S5P pixels — not neighbourhood detail.

Column ≠ personal exposure

Tropospheric column density, not ground/personal exposure.

Cloud gaps

Monsoon months have fewer clear scenes after masking.

Modelled population

WorldPop ~100 m residential estimates, not census microdata.

Boundary: dataset/Mumbai_boundary.geojson (GREATER MUMBAI / 802794) from the urban ULB release. Notebook: question2/trial.ipynb.
