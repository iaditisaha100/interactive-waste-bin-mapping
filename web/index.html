What the survey shows

There are 47 point observations from one walk around campus. The points run from the south gate and faculty quarters through the grocery, cafeteria, lecture hall complex, and labs, and up to the sports complex, Malhar and Des hostels, and the north mess. 25 notes describe bins that were not segregated. Dry-waste and wet-waste bins show up mainly around the grocery, the cafeteria, and the paths toward the hostels and the north mess. 4 records have no title or note. Two further notes stay in their own group: a dumping area recorded as “Too much garbage”, and one lab bin labelled only “Waste bin”.
